FocusFlow Audio Directory
=========================
You can place audio tracks in your FocusFlow local audio folder (via Settings -> Open Audio Folder).
To use your own Soft White Noise track, name it 'soft-white-noise.mp3' (or a supported audio format) and refresh your library in Settings.

Supported formats:
- MP3 (.mp3)
- WAV (.wav)
- OGG (.ogg)
- FLAC (.flac)
- M4A (.m4a)
- AAC (.aac)
